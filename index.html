Our Picks

6 must-try spots hand-picked by Zhangjiajie locals

🏆 #1 Must-Try Lunch / Dinner
李憨憨三下锅 Li Hanhan's Three-Pot Stew

三下锅 (Sān Xià Guō) — Zhangjiajie's signature dish. A sizzling dry wok of smoked pork belly, tripe, and fresh meat in a bold, spicy-fragrant sauce. The smokiness from the cured meat is unforgettable. Li Hanhan's version is beloved for its generous portions and authentic flavor.

🗺️ 永定区新松机器人欢乐城1层 💰 ¥50-80 (~$7-11) ⏰ 11:00-21:00
🌶️🌶️🌶️ Spicy — ask for 微辣 (wēi là) for mild

"Li Hanhan's is the real deal. Generous portions, bold flavors, no shortcuts. This is the three-pot stew we grew up with."

— Xiao Li Local

"I almost went to KFC that night. This place changed my entire trip. The flavors are incredible — nothing like this back home."

— James T., United Kingdom Tourist
❤️ Local Favorite Lunch / Dinner
北站胖嫂打鼓皮 North Station Fat Aunt's Drum Skin

打鼓皮 (Dǎ Gǔ Pí) — A beloved Zhangjiajie specialty! Crispy, chewy strips of ox tripe stir-fried with fresh chili, garlic, and local spices. The texture is addictive — crunchy on the outside, tender inside. Fat Aunt's version is the local gold standard.

🗺️ 永定区西溪坪街道打谷台民安2期 💰 ¥40-60 (~$6-8) ⏰ 11:00-21:00
🌶️🌶️🌶️ Spicy — the chili enhances the crispy texture

"打鼓皮 is THE Zhangjiajie snack. Fat Aunt does it best — perfectly crispy every time. A perfect pairing with cold beer!"

— Brother Chen Local

"I had no idea what 'drum skin' was but it turned out to be the crunchiest, most flavorful tripe I've ever eaten. A must-try!"

— Sarah M., Australia Tourist
🌅 Breakfast King Breakfast
刘聋子粉馆 Liu Longzi Noodle House

湖南米粉 (Húnán Mǐfěn) — Authentic Hunan-style rice noodles in a rich, slow-simmered broth with your choice of toppings. Liu Longzi is a legendary name in Zhangjiajie — the broth is cooked for hours and the noodles are perfectly silky. Get there early, locals queue up before dawn.

🗺️ 永定区回龙路商业步行街1094号 💰 ¥10-18 (~$1.5-3) ⏰ 6:00-14:00
🌶️ Mild — add chili oil yourself at the table

"Liu Longzi is a Zhangjiajie institution. If you don't eat rice noodles for breakfast here, did you even visit?"

— Uncle Liu Local

"$2 for the best breakfast I've had in all of China. The broth is insanely flavorful. I went back three mornings in a row."

— Tom R., Canada Tourist
🎭 Hunan Home Cooking Lunch / Dinner
老农村家常菜馆 Old Village Home-Style Kitchen

湘菜 (Xiāng Cài) — Authentic Hunan home-style cooking at its finest. Think wok-fired pork with smoked chili, stir-fried seasonal greens, steamed fish head — all bursting with bold, rustic flavors. Old Village serves the kind of food Hunan grandmas are proud of. No frills, just incredible taste.

🗺️ 永定区回龙路东门巷10号 💰 ¥40-80 (~$6-11) ⏰ 10:30-21:00
🌶️🌶️🌶️ Spicy — real Hunan heat, ask for 微辣 if needed

"This is real Hunan cooking — the kind you get at a local family's dinner table. Every dish tastes like home."

— Teacher Wang Local

"We ordered four dishes and every single one was incredible. The smoked pork with chili literally made me tear up — in the best way."

— Hans K., Germany Tourist
☀️ Summer Essential Snack / Lunch
猪脑壳凉面 Pig Head Cold Noodles

凉面 (Liáng Miàn) — Chilled wheat noodles tossed with sesame paste, chili oil, crushed peanuts, and crunchy toppings. Don't let the quirky name fool you — 猪脑壳 is a Zhangjiajie legend. Their secret sauce recipe has kept locals coming back for years. The ultimate refresher after a sweaty hike.

🗺️ 永定区南正街212号 💰 ¥8-15 (~$1-2) ⏰ 10:00-20:00
🌶️🌶️ Adjustable — tell them your spice level

"猪脑壳 has the best cold noodles in Zhangjiajie, hands down. Their sauce recipe is legendary — no one else comes close."

— Xiao Chen Local

"After hiking Tianmen Mountain in 35°C heat, this was the most perfect meal imaginable. The name is hilarious too. Only $1.50!"

— Yuki S., Japan Tourist
🔥 Social Feast Dinner / Late Night
厂花翠翠 Factory Flower Cuicui

火锅 (Huǒ Guō) — A bubbling cauldron of rich, spicy broth where you cook fresh meats, vegetables, tofu, and noodles right at your table. 厂花翠翠 is famous for their flavorful base soup and ultra-fresh ingredients. The perfect communal dining experience — bring friends!

🗺️ 永定区南门口美食街 💰 ¥60-120 (~$8-17) ⏰ 11:00-23:00
🌶️🌶️🌶️ Spicy — get 鴛鸯锅 (half-spicy) if you want both mild and spicy

"厂花翠翠's broth is next level. We come here every week — it's become our family tradition."

— Sister Mei Local

"Hot pot with locals is the best cultural experience in China. The staff helped us order and we had the most amazing meal. Unforgettable!"

— Marco L., Italy Tourist

Useful Phrases at Restaurants

🗣️

微辣 (Wēi Là)

"Mild spice" — Say this to turn down the heat on any dish.

🗣️

好吃! (Hǎo Chī!)

"Delicious!" — Use this and the chef will love you forever.

🗣️

买单 (Mǎi Dān)

"Check please" — Raise your hand and say this when you're done.

🗣️

不要辣 (Bú Yào Là)

"No spice" — For when your taste buds need a break.
